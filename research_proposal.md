# RESEARCH PROPOSAL

## Research Title

An Analytical Study of Social Media Usage Patterns and Their Influence on Academic Performance Among College Students.

## Research Domain

Data Science and Social Computing.

## Research Type

Survey-Based Quantitative Research.

## Project Overview

This research aims to study social media usage patterns among college students and understand how their usage relates to academic performance, concentration, and learning activities.

The study will involve collecting survey responses from college students and analysing the collected data using Python and Data Science techniques. The findings will be presented through statistical analysis, graphs, and visualizations.

A web-based prototype will also be developed to display the research findings through interactive dashboards.
## Problem Statement

Social media has become an important part of college students' daily lives. Students use different social media platforms for communication, entertainment, education, and information sharing. However, excessive or unplanned usage may be associated with challenges such as reduced study time, lack of concentration, and changes in academic habits.

There is a need to understand how college students use social media and examine the relationship between their usage patterns and academic performance. This research aims to analyse these patterns using survey data and statistical techniques.

## Research Objectives

1. To identify the most commonly used social media platforms among college students.
2. To analyse the amount of time students spend on social media daily.
3. To examine the relationship between social media usage and academic performance.
4. To understand how social media usage relates to students' concentration and productivity.
5. To identify the educational benefits and challenges of social media usage.
6. To present the research findings through data visualization and an interactive web-based prototype.
## Research Questions

1. Which social media platforms are most frequently used by college students?
2. How much time do college students spend on social media each day?
3. Is there a relationship between social media usage and academic performance?
4. How does social media usage relate to students' concentration and study habits?
5. Do students use social media for educational and academic purposes?
6. What are the major benefits and challenges of social media usage among college students?

## Proposed Research Methodology

This study will follow a survey-based quantitative research approach.

### 1. Data Collection

Primary data will be collected from college students using a structured questionnaire created through Google Forms.

### 2. Target Population

The target population will consist of college students from different academic years and streams.

### 3. Data Processing

The collected survey responses will be organized and processed using Python libraries such as Pandas and NumPy.

### 4. Data Analysis

Statistical techniques will be used to analyse social media usage patterns, daily screen time, academic activities, and student responses.

### 5. Data Visualization

Graphs and charts will be created using Matplotlib and Seaborn to present the findings clearly.

### 6. Prototype Development

A web-based interactive dashboard will be developed using Streamlit to display the analysed results.
## Scope of Research

This research focuses on analysing social media usage patterns among college students. It covers the commonly used social media platforms, daily usage duration, purposes of usage, study habits, concentration, and academic performance.

The study will collect responses from college students through an online questionnaire. The collected data will be analysed using statistical methods and data visualization techniques.

The research will focus on identifying usage patterns and examining possible relationships between social media usage and academic activities. The findings will be limited to the students who participate in the survey and may not represent all college students.

## Expected Outcomes

1. Identification of the most popular social media platforms among college students.
2. Understanding of students' daily social media usage patterns.
3. Insights into the relationship between social media usage and academic performance.
4. Identification of educational benefits and challenges associated with social media.
5. Visual representation of survey findings through graphs and charts.
6. Development of an interactive web-based dashboard to present research findings.
## Research Methodology

### 1. Research Design

This study will use a quantitative research approach to examine social media usage patterns and their relationship with academic activities among college students.

### 2. Data Collection

Primary data will be collected through a structured questionnaire. The questionnaire will focus on social media usage duration, commonly used platforms, purposes of usage, study habits and students' self-reported academic experiences.

### 3. Target Population

The target population will consist of college students from different academic years and streams. The final sample size and participant selection method will be decided before data collection.

### 4. Data Processing

The collected responses will be organised and analysed using Python libraries such as Pandas and NumPy. The data will be checked for missing or inconsistent responses.

### 5. Data Analysis

Statistical techniques will be used to identify patterns and relationships between social media usage and academic activities.

### 6. Data Visualization

Charts and graphs will be created using Matplotlib and Seaborn to present the analysis clearly.

### 7. Prototype Development

An interactive dashboard will be developed using Streamlit. It will display survey statistics, usage patterns and important research findings.

### 8. Ethical Considerations

Participation will be voluntary. Responses will be kept anonymous, and personal identifying information will not be collected. Appropriate institutional approval and consent procedures will be followed.

# DATA ANALYSIS PLAN

## 1. Objective

To analyse social media usage patterns among college students and examine their relationship with academic activities.

## 2. Data Requirements

The study will require survey responses containing information about:

* Academic year and field of study.
* Social media platforms commonly used.
* Daily social media usage duration.
* Main purpose of social media usage.
* Social media usage during study hours.
* Students' self-reported academic experiences.

## 3. Tools and Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Streamlit

## 4. Analysis Techniques

* Frequency analysis
* Percentage calculation
* Average usage analysis
* Comparison across academic years
* Relationship analysis between usage duration and academic habits

## 5. Expected Visualizations

* Bar chart of popular social media platforms.
* Pie chart of social media usage purposes.
* Bar chart of daily usage duration.
* Comparison of study habits across usage groups.
* Summary dashboard of important findings.

## 6. Expected Output

The analysis will identify common social media usage patterns and explore their relationship with students' academic activities. Results will be based on the actual survey responses collected.
